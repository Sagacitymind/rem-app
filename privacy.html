Rem privacy policy

Rem is a personal assistant used only by its owners on their own computer.

  • Rem accesses Google account data (such as Gmail) only for the owners' own accounts and only to help them with their own email.
  • Data is processed and stored only on the owners' own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Rem's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.
